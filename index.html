Home / Water Storage

Save your pool water during renovations

Instead of draining your pool, we store the water in collapsible tanks while we work, then return it. Ideal for conserving water, especially during drought.

200,000 LMaximum storage capacity
20 million+ LOf water saved from the drain
About 1 weekTypical duration

How it works

  1. Site inspection

    Our team checks that your garden or yard can take the tank.

  2. Tank setup

    The collapsible tank is set up and your pool water is pumped into it.

  3. Renovation

    Renovation or cleaning carries on without interruption.

  4. Water returned

    Your water goes back into the pool. The process typically takes about a week.

Gentle on your property

The tank's weight is spread over a large area to minimise any impact on your driveway or lawn. Water is stored with no evaporation.

A surcharge applies for the use of the tanks.
A site inspection is required before setup.
